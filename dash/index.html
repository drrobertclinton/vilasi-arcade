How to play

  • Blip runs by itself. Tap, click or press Space to jump. Hold to keep hopping.
  • Land on blocks. Touch a spike or crash into a wall and you restart.
  • Pads on the floor launch you high. Rings in the air give a second jump if you tap inside them.
  • Fly through a wave gate and Blip becomes a dart: hold to fly up, let go to dive. Don't touch the walls.
  • Other gates change how Blip moves. Fly: hold to rise. Ball: tap to roll onto the ceiling and back. UFO: every tap is a hop, even in the air. Robot: hold longer to jump higher. Spider: tap to zip to the other side. Swing: each tap flips which way you fall.
  • Orbs: yellow jumps, pink jumps a little, red jumps big, green flips you and jumps, black slams you down. Blue orbs flip you just by touching them. Pads: yellow launches high, pink launches low, blue flips you.
  • Speed gates with arrows make Blip slower or faster: one arrow is slow, four is very fast.
  • Practice mode (pick it on the level screen or in the pause menu) drops green checkpoints as you go, so you restart from the last one. Z adds a checkpoint, X removes the last one. Practice runs don't count toward stars or orbs.
  • Grab the 3 hidden ★ stars in each level.
  • R or the ↻ button starts the level over. Esc or P pauses.
